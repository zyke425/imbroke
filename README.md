## Presenter controls

- Navigate with the footer arrows, Left/Right Arrow, Page Up/Down, or Space. Home and End jump to the ends. Numeric hashes such as `#15` open that slide. Rule menu links use stable slide IDs.
- Press **E** or **Explain This Slide** to start a manual cat explanation. Use **Next explanation**, **Previous explanation**, **Replay**, and **Stop**. The caption, step count, highlight, and reveal move together. **Full transcript** is available below the caption. **S** or Escape stops.
- **Automatic explanations** and **Voice narration** are optional and off by default. Auto mode waits for reading time and voice completion. Captions work without browser speech.
- The tools menu offers Symbol guide, keyboard help, Focus mode, fullscreen, and printing. **O** opens the slide overview, **F** toggles fullscreen, and **?** shows keyboard help.
- Cat jokes are off in a fresh session; saved preferences are honored. Enable jokes and choose **Tell a short joke** at a presenter chosen break. Jokes are suppressed during explanations.
- Slides 6–10 reveal rule conclusions. Slide 11 advances derived steps. Slide 12 opens each fallacy separately. Slide 13 toggles p and q and shows all four truth rows. Slide 15 advances the direct proof. Slide 16 reveals hint and solution separately, accepts typed reasoning, and offers drawing Undo/Clear. Slide 17 gives feedback only for answered questions.

Presenter opened answers and steps remain visible after Stop or slide navigation. Replay restarts the cat at its first step without erasing them. Drawing and typed reasoning remain until the page closes.

## Print

Select **Print / Save as PDF** from the menu. Use landscape orientation and background graphics. The handout is styled for one slide per page and exposes conclusions, proof steps, truth table, practice solution, and identified quiz answers. Check the browser print preview for your paper size.

The project makes no network requests for fonts, scripts, or images. Optional voice depends on the browser and installed voices.
